What dropadot is

Dropadot is an anonymous, location-based map. Each day, anyone can leave one short public message ("a dot"), one targeted message ("a flare"), one cross-continental question ("a cast"), and one drawing on a shared 100-tile canvas ("the realm"). Most content (dots, messages, flares, casts) is deleted at midnight UTC each day. The day's drawing canvas is preserved as a public artwork — anonymous, with no user-identifying data attached.

The site also includes a small art museum: a wall of frames hanging in the open space of the map. Artists may submit a piece for display through a submission form linked from inside the museum.

The museum

By submitting a piece to the museum, you confirm that you are the artist or otherwise have the right to share the work, and you grant dropadot permission to display the submitted image and any text you provide (your name, title, year, a short bio, and an optional link) on the site as part of the museum.

Museum submissions are not part of the daily reset. A piece may stay on the wall for an extended period — there is no fixed display duration, and we may take any submission down at any time without notice. If you want your own piece removed, contact us at the email below and we will take it down.

The submission form is hosted by Google Forms, so anything you enter passes through Google's servers before reaching us. Please do not submit work you would not want shown publicly.

Location

Dropadot uses an approximate version of your location to place your dot on the map. Your browser shares your full GPS coordinates with this site, but the moment we receive them we round them to about 100 meters of precision before doing anything else with them. We never store or transmit your exact GPS coordinates. You can decline location access, in which case you cannot drop a dot or shoot a flare, but you can still browse and read everything.
