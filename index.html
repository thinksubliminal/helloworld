Dots
Drop one per day at your exact location. Say something to the world. How you feel, what you're thinking, anything. Anyone can reply in short bursts. No back-to-back from the same person; wait for someone else to chime in.
Flares
Aim one anywhere on the map. Anyone on that continent can chime in. The thread closes once enough has been said. One flare per day.
Stars
Take the rocket to space and plant a star anywhere. No dot needed. Anyone who's planted a star today can reply. Same back-and-forth rules as dots. One star per day.
The Spirit
The world is reading. Be kind. No hate, no doxxing, no spam. Anything that breaks the spirit of this place will come down.
Everything resets at midnight UTC.
